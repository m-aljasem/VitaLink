VitaLink lets you record health readings and share them with people you choose, such as your doctor, nurse, family or caregivers. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Who we are

VitaLink is provided by [legal name / organisation], [address]. Contact: [privacy contact email].

What we collect

DataWhy
Email addressTo sign you in with a one-time code.
Profile: name, age, gender, country, city, language, height, health conditions, unit preferences; for providers, provider type and hospitalTo personalise the app, show your name to people you share with, and pick sensible units and date formats.
Health readings: blood pressure, glucose, oxygen saturation, heart rate, pain and weight, with time, tags and your notesThe core purpose of the app: to track your health and share it if you choose.
Medications, doses taken or skipped, and remindersTo remind you and track how regularly you take your medications.
Connections with providers and what you share with eachTo control who can see which of your readings.
A log of when a connected provider viewed your dataSo you can see who looked at your data, under Settings → Access history.
Alerts sent to your providers when you log an out-of-range readingSo the people caring for you can respond.

We do not use advertising, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics, and we do not sell your data.

Where your data is stored

Your account data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, in [region, e.g. EU (Frankfurt)]. It is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by the hosting provider. Database rules ensure each person can only read their own data and what others have chosen to share with them.

A copy of your readings is also kept on your device so the app works offline. It is deleted from the device when you log out.

Who can see your data

  • You.
  • Providers you connect with, using a one-time code they give you. They see only the metrics you switch on for them, and you can change this or remove them at any time under Connect.
  • Service providers that run the app for us (database hosting and email delivery), only as needed to provide the service.
  • Authorities, only if required by law.

Health information

Ranges such as "High" or "Very low" are general adult guidelines to help you notice readings that may need attention. They are not a diagnosis. VitaLink is not a medical device and does not replace professional care. In an emergency, contact your local emergency number.

Your rights and choices

  • Access and export: Settings → Export Data (CSV, JSON, FHIR or PDF).
  • Correct: edit your profile and readings in the app.
  • Control sharing: change or revoke each provider's access under Connect.
  • Delete: see below.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to object or to complain to a data protection authority.

Deleting your account

In the app: open Settings → Delete account and confirm. Your account and all associated data (profile, readings, notes, medications, reminders, provider connections, access logs and alerts) are permanently deleted immediately.

Without the app: email [privacy contact email] from the address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

Copies already exported by you, or by a provider while they had access, are outside our control.

How long we keep data

We keep your data until you delete it or your account. Database backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten within [backup retention period].

Children

VitaLink is not intended for children under 16 to use on their own. A parent or guardian may use it to track a child's health.

Changes
